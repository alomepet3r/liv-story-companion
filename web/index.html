In plain words

Who Liv is: Liv is an AI companion — a computer program, not a human being. She listens, remembers what you share here, and responds — but she is not a person.

What is kept: the words of our conversation, as text, so they can be arranged into a chapter for you to review — and anything you choose to share along the way: a photo, your voice, a video. In this demo, they are kept only while this tab stays open — when you close the tab, this conversation is gone.

Who can see it: no one else. Nothing leaves this conversation unless you approve it first.

Stopping: you may pause, skip a question, or finish at any time — no reason needed.

Who this is for: Liv is for adults (18+) only. Companion chatbots may not be suitable for some minors, so if you are under 18, please do not use this page. And if you tell Liv during your conversation that you are under 18, she will gently have to end the session there.

Three honest things: nothing you share here is legally privileged. If a court orders us to produce records, the law can require us to keep and share what you have told us, even after you delete it. And if you tell Liv a child or vulnerable adult is being hurt, we may need to pass that to people whose job is to protect them.

Your stories are yours, always. You can delete anything, anytime. The only time we could not delete something is if a court, or the law protecting a child, required us to keep it — and then we would keep it locked away and let you know.
