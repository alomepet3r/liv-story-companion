In plain words

What is kept: the words of our conversation, as text, so they can be arranged into a chapter for you to review.

Who can see it: no one else. Nothing leaves this conversation unless you approve it first.

Stopping: you may pause, skip a question, or finish at any time — no reason needed.

Who this is for: Liv is for adults (18+) only at this time.

Yours. Always. Delete anything, anytime.
